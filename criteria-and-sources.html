Financial assumptions

Acquisition costs18.65% of asking price
Renovation estimatePriority: your figure in overrides.json, then a budget supplied by the source, then a works estimate stated in the listing, then the model. Model = living area x condition rate (EUR/m2: new 0, renovated 0, good 60, refresh 250, renovate 650, heavy 1200) + energy upgrade by PEB label (EUR/m2: A 0, B 0, C 30, D 80, E 140, F 200, G 260; x0.5 when the condition is 'renovate' or 'heavy') + oil heating replacement 15,000 EUR + single glazing 12,000 EUR + non-compliant electrics 70 EUR/m2, then 15% contingency and 6% VAT (21% under 10 years). Unknown condition: 'refresh'; unknown PEB: 'E'. Placeholder rates to calibrate with quotes.
Holiday-let fit-out2,000 EUR per bedroom + 6,000 EUR common areas + 5,000 EUR fire safety, included in the total investment.
Operating costs (base case)Platform commission 15% of revenue; cleaning 180 EUR per stay of 2.5 nights; energy = living area x PEB kWh/m2 (default 350) x 0.5 x 0.12 EUR/kWh; property tax = cadastral income x 2.2 x 50% (0.4% of price when unknown); insurance 1,400, water/internet/waste 1,800, other 800 EUR/year; maintenance 1% of price.
FinancingLoan of 80% of the asking price at 3.6% over 20 years (monthly annuity). Purchase costs, works and fit-out come from own funds. Cash flow is before income tax.
Revenue modelMunicipal nightly-rate and occupancy benchmarks, adjusted by guest capacity, living area, bathrooms and known amenities. Default fallback: 375 EUR/night at 35% occupancy.
Gross yieldEstimated annual revenue divided by total investment, before operating costs, financing and tax.
